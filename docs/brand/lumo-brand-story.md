# Lumo Brand Story

Lumo was created around a simple idea:

**Sometimes, when everything feels too loud, you do not need more pressure. You need a little light.**

The name **Lumo** comes from that feeling. It is meant to feel soft, warm, bright, and reassuring — like a small glow in the middle of an overwhelming day.

Lumo is not about forcing productivity, fixing your life, or pushing you to do more. It is about helping you find the next manageable step when your brain feels crowded, your energy is low, or everything is competing for your attention at once.

Lumo exists to soften the noise rather than add to it.

It is built for people who become overloaded easily, especially people who struggle with attention, executive function, emotional regulation, transitions, or the sheer mental weight of keeping track of everyday life.

Instead of using urgency, guilt, streak pressure, or endless notifications, Lumo is designed around clarity, calm, flexibility, and kindness.

The goal is not perfection.

The goal is to make life feel a little more possible.

---

## The Light and the Weather

At the heart of Lumo is a simple metaphor:

**Lumo is the light. Miri is the weather.**

Life does not feel the same every day.

Some days are clear. Some are foggy. Some feel heavy, chaotic, stormy, or impossible to organise.

Lumo is not designed around the idea that those difficult days need to be corrected before you can function.

It does not try to fix the weather.

It gives you a small glow inside it.

That glow might be:

- a clear next task
- a gentle reminder
- a simplified view
- permission to do less
- reassurance that small progress still counts
- a way to start again without shame

Lumo helps you navigate the day you actually have rather than demanding the day you were supposed to have.

---

## Meet Miri

**Miri is Lumo’s cloud companion.**

Miri represents overwhelmed humans in the gentlest possible way: soft, expressive, occasionally chaotic, but still floating.

The character reflects the internal weather many people experience while trying to manage:

- work
- family
- appointments
- money
- health
- routines
- emotions
- responsibilities
- everything else happening at once

Miri makes Lumo feel human rather than clinical.

They are not a coach, authority figure, or productivity mascot standing over you waiting for results.

Miri is simply there beside you.

They appear when the experience needs a little more warmth:

- when something feels difficult
- when you complete something small
- when plans change
- when you need to reset
- when you are overwhelmed
- when you are focusing
- when you are trying again

Miri never judges your pace.

Miri never treats an unfinished task as a personal failure.

Miri just keeps floating with you.

---

## Why the Name Miri

The name **Miri** was chosen because it feels small, gentle, and affectionate.

It carries the feeling of a tiny **mini-miracle** appearing in the middle of an ordinary day — something little that makes things feel lighter without pretending everything is suddenly easy.

That is what Miri represents inside Lumo.

Not a dramatic transformation.

Just a little softness when you need it.

---

## What Lumo Believes

Lumo is built around a few core beliefs.

### People should not need to feel ashamed before they are allowed to get organised.

A planning tool should help reduce pressure, not create another source of it.

### A difficult day does not mean you are failing.

Energy, attention, emotion, and capacity change from day to day.

The system should be able to change with you.

### A task system should reduce cognitive load.

It should make decisions easier, not create another complicated system to maintain.

### Small actions still count.

Rest, recovery, preparation, restarting, and tiny steps are meaningful progress.

### Planning should adapt to the person.

The person should not have to reshape themselves to fit the planner.

### Support does not have to be loud.

Sometimes the most helpful thing is simply:

**Here is the next step. You can start there.**

## The Role of Miri

Miri supports the emotional layer of the product.

The interface itself should remain clear, functional, and calm.

Miri appears selectively, when their presence adds reassurance, recognition, warmth, or personality.

Miri can:

- celebrate a completed task without turning productivity into a performance
- appear during an empty state so the screen does not feel like failure
- offer reassurance when something has gone wrong
- make reminders feel gentler
- acknowledge effort
- support a reset
- appear during focus and planning moments
- soften difficult or overwhelming states
- celebrate small wins

Different Miri states represent different kinds of internal weather.

None of those states are presented as good or bad.

They are simply states.

And states change.

## Miri Behaviour Principles

Miri should never become noisy or intrusive.

Miri should not:

- appear on every screen
- interrupt task completion
- dominate important UI
- create urgency
- guilt the user
- look disappointed
- react negatively to unfinished tasks
- imply that productivity determines worth
- become visually distracting during focus states

Miri should:

- appear contextually
- remain visually secondary to the task
- reinforce effort rather than perfection
- acknowledge progress
- soften errors and setbacks
- create emotional continuity throughout the product
- feel like a companion, not a supervisor

---

## Voice and Tone

Lumo should feel:

- calm
- warm
- clear
- supportive
- optimistic
- flexible
- human
- gentle
- reassuring

Lumo should never feel:

- judgmental
- demanding
- urgent without reason
- patronising
- clinical
- guilt-driven
- overly cheerful when someone is struggling
- obsessed with streaks or productivity metrics

The voice should acknowledge difficulty without making it heavier.

It should encourage without demanding.

It should help without hovering.

---

## Language Principles

Prefer language like:

- “One step at a time.”
- “You can come back to this.”
- “A little progress still counts.”
- “Want to make this smaller?”
- “Here’s what matters most right now.”
- “You tried.”
- “That’s enough for today.”
- “Ready when you are.”
- “Plans can change.”
- “Let’s make this easier.”

Avoid language like:

- “You failed.”
- “You missed your goal.”
- “You broke your streak.”
- “You should have…”
- “Don’t forget!”
- “You’re falling behind.”
- “Be productive.”
- “No excuses.”
- “Complete everything today.”

---

## Brand Metaphor

The central metaphor behind Lumo should remain consistent across product, copy, illustration, marketing, and brand decisions:

**Lumo is the light. Miri is the weather.**

The weather can change.

The light remains available.

Lumo is not there to remove every difficult feeling, eliminate every chaotic day, or force someone into constant productivity.

It is there to help them find their way through whatever the day looks like.

---

## The Promise

Lumo is not here to tell you to get your life together.

It is here to make the next part feel a little easier.

Miri will not clear every cloud.

Lumo will not make every day simple.

That is not the promise.

The promise is smaller, gentler, and more useful:

**When the day feels overwhelming, Lumo gives you a little light to move by.**
